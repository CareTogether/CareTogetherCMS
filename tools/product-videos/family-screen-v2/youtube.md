**Description:**

A quick tour of the redesigned Family screen in CareTogether.

In this video:

- The ⋮ menu for less frequent actions, like printing notes

**Description:**

How to add notes and approve draft notes on the new Family screen in CareTogether.

In this video:
- Add a note from any tab with the Note button

## Video 3: `video-3-requirements.mp4`

**Title:** CareTogether: Complete a Volunteer Requirement

**Description:**

How to complete, exempt, and track volunteer requirements on the new Family screen in CareTogether.

In this video:
- See how many requirements are missing on the Approvals tab
- Open a role card to see the requirements for the whole family and for each person
- Complete a missing requirement, attach a document, and leave a note
- See what an exemption looks like, with its reason and end date
- Log any other action with Complete Other
- Filter by Expiring to plan renewals before they lapse
