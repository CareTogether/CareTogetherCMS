# Examples

Read these for the target voice. They come from a product announcement that a human liked after rewriting, so copy the moves, not the topic.

## Good moves

**Cut the selling, keep the fact**

Before: A header that does the common work. The family's name, roles, phone, email, and address are at the top.
After: Header. The header displays the name of the family, roles, phone number, email and address.

**Fragments for lists of contents**

Before: Documents: every file uploaded for the family, newest first, with download.
After: Documents. All documents uploaded with the newest on top. Can be downloaded.

**Turn a descriptive sentence into short task fragments**

Before: Open a role, complete a missing requirement with a document, see an exemption, use Complete Other, and filter for requirements that are expiring.
After: Opening up a role. Completing a missing requirement by uploading a document. See exemptions. Using Complete Other. Filtering expiring requirements.

**One instruction per sentence**

Before: Click your profile picture in the top bar, choose Beta Features, and turn off Family Screen V2.
After: Click on your profile picture in the top bar. Select Beta Features. Switch off Family Screen V2.

**Plain words for symbols and framing**

Before: The ⋮ menu has the less frequent actions.
After: The 3 dots button has less frequently used actions.

## Mistakes to avoid

The same rewrite introduced these errors. All of them read fine, which is why step 1 exists.

| Source | Bad rewrite | Problem |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arrangements (client families) or Assignments (volunteer families) | Arrangements/Assignments (Volunteers only) | Lost the pairing, wrong audience |
| shows how many items are missing or expired | how many requirements are missing or expiring | Expired became expiring |
| removing a role | deleting a role | Different action |
| all cases, including closed ones | including those that have been completed | Closed became completed |
| link organizations | adding organisations | Different action |
| find and download them | Searching for and downloading | Find became search |
| opens in a panel, so you don't lose your place | will open up a side panel | Dropped the reason for the feature |
| at the top / below, inside the panel | top of the screen / bottom | Invented a location |
| We're rolling out | We have implemented | Changed a gradual rollout into a finished one |

Also avoid an unearned "However" at the start of a sentence, and do not change US spelling to UK spelling or the reverse.
